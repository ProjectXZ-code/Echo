Everything a local player should be

Built for people who keep their music on-device — FLAC, hi-res, Atmos, and the rest of your library.

🎧

Hi-Res & lossless

FLAC, ALAC, WAV and more with clear Hi-Res / Lossless badges on the player and library.

🔊

Dolby Atmos

Detects Atmos / multichannel tracks, bypasses processing, and hands the stream to the system spatializer.

🚗

Android Auto + Bluetooth

Browse songs, albums, artists, and playlists in the car. Steering-wheel controls over Bluetooth AVRCP.

📝

Synced lyrics

Full-screen lyrics, select up to 10 lines, and share story-ready cards to Instagram and more.

📊

Listening stats & Wrapped

Spotify-style minutes for today, month, and year — plus a shareable year-end Wrapped.

🖼️

Artist photos

Auto-fetched artist images for the library and artist pages, with album art as a reliable fallback.

🎚️

EQ & spatial

Equalizer, remaster tools for older recordings, and immersive spatial modes when you want them.

🔒

Lock screen player

Rich controls and artwork on the lock screen, plus widgets for the home screen.

📱

Material you UI

Jetpack Compose interface with light / dark themes and a player designed for focus while listening.

Made for the car

USB or wireless Android Auto, plus classic Bluetooth head units.
